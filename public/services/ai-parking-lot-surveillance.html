Mobile AI Security

A Self-Contained Security Tower, Anywhere You Need It

The ISG AI Surveillance Trailer is a complete security post on wheels. A telescoping mast lifts HD AI-powered PTZ cameras high above your site for a commanding view, while onboard artificial intelligence watches every frame and decides what actually deserves attention. It knows the difference between a person climbing a fence and a branch swaying in the wind, so shadows, weather and animals don’t trigger alerts.

When the AI spots a real threat, the trailer responds on its own: bright strobe lights and a loud siren let intruders know they’ve been seen, and an alert with live video goes straight to the ISG team. From there we can verify the activity, notify you, dispatch a mobile patrol unit, or call law enforcement.

Everything runs on solar power with battery backup and a built-in cellular connection, so there is nothing to trench, wire or plug in. And when your needs change, we can reposition the trailer on your property or relocate it to another site entirely.
